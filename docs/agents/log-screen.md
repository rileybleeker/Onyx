## Log screen (2026-11-15)

`/log` (nav "Log", G 1, Quick access with Today, the phone bar's centre Log tab) is where Riley makes every daily entry on one screen (Riley: "make it really easy for me to do all my entries from one screen"). Frame: `app/log/page.tsx` (static shell, title "Log") → `LogLoader.tsx` (`dynamic(ssr:false)`, skeleton `LogSkeleton.tsx` painted by the server) → `LogClient.tsx`: the h1 "Log", a Meal photo link (`/nutrition?log=photo`, what the old quick-log sheet offered besides the pages below) and ONE `<PartGrid page="/log">` whose parts are the sections, in this default order: `say-it`, `check-in` (nested), `wind-down`, `journal`, `supplements`, `uber-eats`, `weight` (half), `reading` (half). **One file per section** in `app/log/sections/` (`SayIt`, `CheckIn`, `WindDown`, `Journal`, `Supplements`, `UberEats`, `Weight`, `Reading`), each bundled with the page's own first scripts (LogLoader imports `LogClient`, which imports every section, so the HTML loads all of the screen's code in parallel with the framework, with no chunk round trip after hydration; each keeps its `dynamic()` wrapper and skeleton; a hidden part is never mounted; their first reads start with the HTML, see the speed-budget bullet), loading with `SectionSkeleton` from `sections/ui.tsx` (also `LogCard`, `DraftMark`, `LogSaveButton`), reading its data from the client cache first and writing ONLY through the existing routes with their existing validation and date rules, ONLY after Riley's own input or his Say it words (autosave, `lib/autosave.ts`: no Save buttons; never on open, never on scroll). The Check-in section is the real `CheckInClient` (`feed embedded`, inside `<InFeed>` so its own grid never claims Personalize; its own autosave, drafts and rules), not a copy; `omitParts` leaves out its "say-it-once" / "journal-draft" / "uber-eats" / "uber-eats-leftovers" parts once `REPLACES_CHECKIN_SAY_IT_ONCE` (SayIt.tsx) / `REPLACES_CHECKIN_JOURNAL_DRAFT` (Journal.tsx, true) / `REPLACES_CHECKIN_UBER_EATS` (UberEats.tsx) are true; without its journal card, the journal draft of the check-in's own Say it once goes to the Journal section as a `journal` draft (words handed over by the Log Say it box get none from the check-in: that box drafts its own). `/check-in` stays its own page for the 21:30 card's deep links, but has no FeedSection: the scroll shows the check-in once, inside Log. **Drafts (`lib/log-drafts.ts`):** the Say it box writes suggestions with `setLogDrafts({...}, said)`; each section reads `useLogDrafts(section)` (`checkin` = `{text}`, run through the check-in's own read-only voice fill; `supplements` = product or stack + amount + times + date; `ubereats` = portion or leftover per `item_id`; `weight` = lb + date; `reading` = book + minutes + chapters + medium + recall + date; `journal` = body VERBATIM + source + optional title / mood / confidence / topics + `asked`; exact types in the file), shows them marked "from what you said" and calls `clear(draft.id)` after its Save succeeded or on dismiss. Memory only: no storage, no request, gone on reload; a draft never overwrites what Riley tapped. Warm-up: `/log` in `PINNED`, `DATA` and `CODE` of `AppData.tsx` (a section adds its GET reads to `DATA["/log"]`). Tests: `frontend/tests/log-drafts.test.cjs`, `frontend/tests/mobile-nav-accessibility.test.cjs`, smoke `/log`. **Wind-down** (`sections/WindDown.tsx` and the Today panel, PROD-SLEEP-01, opt-in): rules, storage and saving are in `docs/sleep-winddown.md`.
- **Log: Weight and Reading (2026-11-15, `app/log/sections/Weight.tsx`, `Reading.tsx`).** Weight shows today's row from `GET /api/weight?days=365` (a year, so the last weigh-in still shows after a long gap) with its source (Tanita / Manual), else the latest; one pounds field (16 px, no iOS zoom) + Day (ET calendar day, max today) that **save themselves** (`lib/weight-entry.ts`, 2026-11-15) through `POST /api/weight` with the /nutrition rules (0 to 1000 lb; a day that has a row is replaced and becomes manual, the line under the field says so): 1 s after the last keystroke (4 s when the number is over 30 % from the last weigh-in, a half-typed "18"), at once on Enter, leaving the field or hiding the page; later edits replace the same day; moving the Day after a save moves the entry (the earlier day gets back what it had: a manual row its old value and note, a Tanita or empty day is cleared and the next scale run restores Tanita; a day older than the rows on screen is read first); Undo does the same; an emptied field saves nothing. Reading shows the current book from `GET /api/reading` (same key as Today and /reading): chapters, percent, pace, streak, Kindle position, and that behavioral day's sessions read-only (Kindle ones are the harvester's); below them an Audio / Paper / Other session (from / through chapter with next-chapter chips, minutes, day, recall note stored verbatim) that saves itself: created through `POST /api/reading/sessions` once "Through" is set and Riley pauses or leaves a field, then every edit updates it through `PATCH /api/reading/sessions/[id]`, with "New session" and "Remove this session" (a Say it draft that arrives after a session was saved starts a new one only once the old one is saved; otherwise its words wait, with Use); the day is sent only when chosen, so the server's behavioral today applies. A draft that arrives after a session was saved starts a new one. Both apply their Say it draft to fields Riley has not changed (marked "from what you said", Use / Dismiss when it could not be applied), save it like his own entry and clear it after the save.
- **Log: Journal (2026-11-15, `app/log/sections/Journal.tsx`).** Today's entries already in Onyx (`GET /api/journal/list` for the New York date, `journalTodayUrl` in `lib/journal-file.ts`; titles + mood, each linking to its Notion page; memory-only in the client cache, like every `/api/journal` key), a box for a typed or dictated entry ("Captured by" voice / typed: voice when a dictation inserts a whole phrase at once, typed otherwise, until Riley picks one) that **saves by itself (2026-11-15, no Save button)**: 5 s after typing stops, on leaving the box or when the page is hidden, `POST /api/journal/file` files ONE entry (see the journal bullet under Database: body verbatim, Date today in New York, labels Riley did not set inferred in one model call), then further edits update THAT page through **`PATCH /api/journal/file/[pageId]`** (3 s pause; only the changed paragraphs; Source always; labels re-inferred only when the entry changed by a quarter of its words and 8+ words (`shouldRelabel`), and written only where the page still holds the label this box wrote last). The route is session-only, never in `isCiRoute()`, same-origin JSON with the 32 KB cap, refuses unknown fields, never changes the date, and refuses pages outside the Entries database or in the trash (410 `gone`). Before every send the box writes that version's SHA-256 (a synchronous implementation, so a send from a closing tab is covered), its paragraph hashes and the entry's day into the device copy. Requests carry `known_sha` (the versions filed or sent) and, for the PATCH, `known_paras`. A page holding one of those versions, or only those paragraphs (an update Notion stopped halfway, which the retry finishes), is this box's own; anything else was edited in Notion and is not overwritten (409 `changed`: Riley picks Replace, which sends `force`, or File as a new entry). A restored entry files on its own day, and POST looks for a page of that day by hash with no 15-minute limit (`stale` → the box PATCHes it), so a lost answer never leaves a second entry. "New entry" saves what the box holds first (restored words included) and clears nothing unless that save went through; only Discard drops restored words. Entries filed here are listed under Today at once and reach Journal after the hourly sync. Words not saved yet are kept on the device (`onyx.journal.unsaved`, wiped on sign-out, removed once saved; it holds the words, the entry's day and hashes, never another version's text) and restored after a reload or a closed tab, waiting for Keep or an edit (nothing is written on open or on scroll). A `journal` draft fills an empty box (its labels are sent only while the words are unchanged): a "journal:" note (`asked: true`, set by `sayItJournalDraft`) is filed by itself; a reflection the model picked out, or the check-in's Say it once draft, waits for Keep (or an edit) and is never stored on the device. Over words or a filed entry it is only offered (Add to entry / New entry with these words / Dismiss). For the Say it box, `lib/journal-file.ts` has `sayItJournalDraft(text, modelJournal)` with `SAY_IT_JOURNAL_SCHEMA` / `SAY_IT_JOURNAL_RULES`: a "journal:" prefix makes the rest the entry, otherwise the model's `is_journal` decides and its `excerpt` is used only when it is an exact quote of the note (else the whole note). Tests: `frontend/tests/journal-file.test.cjs`, `frontend/tests/log-drafts.test.cjs`.

**Uber Eats section (`app/log/sections/UberEats.tsx`, 2026-11-15).** The same dishes and controls as `/check-in` (`components/DeliveryPortions.tsx`: the day's delivered restaurant dishes plus the 2 days before still unanswered, "Ate: All / 3/4 / 1/2 / 1/4 / None", leftovers with the time eaten), with a Today / Yesterday / day-before switch (the embedded check-in's day tabs no longer show Uber Eats: `REPLACES_CHECKIN_UBER_EATS = true`, and `CheckInClient` skips its dish read when both parts are omitted; `/check-in` itself is unchanged). Taps save on their own exactly as on `/check-in` (`/nutrition` shows the same lists read-only: `DeliveryDishList` / `LeftoverList` without `onPick`) (PATCH `/api/delivery/items/[id]`, PUT `.../leftovers`, `createPortionSaver`). The line under the heading, "Uber Eats checked N min ago", is the newest `ubereats|items` heartbeat (`getUberEatsCheck`, time + status only), read every minute while the screen is visible; when it moves, the dishes are read again (dishes already on screen stay); over 25 min it says the PC looks off or asleep, a failed run is red with a Status link. Say it drafts (`useLogDrafts("ubereats")`) are drawn dashed "from what you said" on the matching control, listed in a box with Dismiss and stored on their own 0.8 s later (each suggestion tried once; one still waiting gets Retry): portions first, then leftovers (a leftover needs its delivery-day portion below All); a dish Riley tapped since the section loaded keeps his answer; a draft for another day offers "Show <day>" (and switches to it when the draft has nothing for the day on screen and Riley has not picked a day). Speed: `useDeliveryDishes` takes an opt-in `cacheKey` (`deliveryWindowKey`, under `delivery:` so every Uber Eats write drops it and the registered fetcher reads it again) and paints the cached window on the first render; `getDeliveryWindow` reads the leftover rows in parallel with the dishes (by eaten day, one round trip fewer, for every caller); `DATA["/log"]` warms behavioral today, today's window and the heartbeat. Pure parts in `lib/log-ubereats.ts`.

**Say it box (2026-11-15, `sections/SayIt.tsx`, `lib/log-voice.ts`).** Riley types or dictates (the keyboard's microphone) and taps Fill: the words go at once to the embedded check-in as `{checkin: {text}}` (its own read-only fill: daytime answers and last night's sleep, no journal draft for these words; `REPLACES_CHECKIN_SAY_IT_ONCE` is true, so the check-in's own Say it once box is left out on /log) and, in parallel, to **`POST /api/log/voice`** (`{text, date?}`, date = behavioral today by default, the check-in's 3-day window; owner session only, never in `isCiRoute()`; same-origin JSON, 16 KB cap, unknown fields refused; ONE `claude-sonnet-5-5` call, effort low, a static JSON schema so it compiles once; writes nothing, never stores or logs the text). It answers drafts in the shared shapes: journal (`sayItJournalDraft` with `SAY_IT_JOURNAL_SCHEMA` / `SAY_IT_JOURNAL_RULES` from `lib/journal-file.ts`, the model also gets `known_topics`: a "journal:" prefix, or a note the model calls a reflection, gives an entry of the words verbatim with suggested labels); supplements (a product id from the active list, else the chat tool's `resolveProduct` on the said name; amount + unit; "HH:MM" times; date) and stacks by id; Uber Eats portions (the day's dishes and the 2 days before whose portion is unset, the check-in's rule) and leftovers for that day (`leftoverQuestions`, or a dish whose portion the same note sets below All; portions come first in the list; a leftover time still to come is dropped, the guard would refuse it); weight in lb (kg converted, 60 to 600); one reading session (a book being read; the recall is kept only when it is found word for word in the text, `findVerbatim`, never a paraphrase). Plus one readable line per draft (`summary`, shown under the box per section) and `not_matched` (things said that match no list). The model sees only the words and the candidate lists: product and stack names, the last 4 days' dish names with restaurant and portion state, the books being read with chapter counts. A list that fails to load is named in `unavailable` and that section is not filled. An empty section gets no draft (its earlier suggestion stays). The words and the last result live in module memory only (kept while scrolling, gone on reload).

**One screen, joined up (2026-11-15, `app/log/jump.ts`).** Each Log part starts with a zero-height anchor `log-part-<part id>` (`logAnchorId`, `logHref`): `/log#log-part-supplements` opens the screen at that section and keeps it at the top while the sections above grow from their skeletons, until Riley scrolls, touches or types or 2.5 s pass (`jumpToLogPart(part, {settle})`; only the routed page reads the hash, never a copy in the scroll). Today's "Start your check-in", its quick links (Supplement, Habit = the check-in, Weight) and "Open check-in" open these, and the desktop jump list has Log instead of Check-in; `/check-in` stays its own page for the 21:30 card's dated deep links. Under the Say it box, every section a Fill gave something is a row that scrolls to it (a section hidden in Personalize says so instead), and the check-in's row is live: the embedded check-in reports its own fill (`reportCheckinFill` / `useCheckinFill` in `lib/log-drafts.ts`: waiting, the "Filled N answers" line, or why nothing was filled), because its own Say it once box, where that line used to appear, is left out on /log. Embedded, the check-in drops its `max-w-xl` column so it lines up with the other sections. On a touch screen every field on /log reads at 16 px (`.log-screen` rule in `globals.css`), so iOS never zooms the page in on focus, including the shared check-in and Uber Eats controls.

## One place to enter data (2026-11-15)

Riley: "reduce duplicate pages where I can input data." Every daily entry is made on `/log` (check-in and habits, journal, supplements, Uber Eats portions and leftovers, weight, reading sessions). The domain pages keep their charts and history, the management Log cannot do, and corrections, and show a `components/LogLink.tsx` link (`logHref(part)` from `app/log/jump.ts`) where their form used to be:
- `/habits`: no "Today's Habits" checklist or backdate picker ("Answer today's habits on Log" in the header). The heatmap's Yes / No / Clear popover stays for corrections (`/api/habits/complete`, same guards). Habits are still managed in Notion.
- `/supplements`: no tap-to-log on products or stacks and no Intake date / time override; `lib/supplement-writes.ts` (the Saving / Not saved receipts of those taps) went with them. Kept: the library (add, scan, custom product, remove from the list), the stack editor, Today's intakes and Recent intakes with edit / delete (a refused delete stays listed with Retry), compound totals. The add-product step may still log the first dose of a NEW product (Log cannot add products).
- `/nutrition`: Uber Eats dishes and leftovers are read-only (`DeliveryDishList` / `LeftoverList` without `onPick` draw the answer as text: "Ate 3/4", "Ate All (assumed)", "Not answered (counts as thrown out)"); no weight form (Recent entries keeps delete; `/nutrition?log=weight` forwards to `/log#log-part-weight`). The meal photo logger stays here (Log's Meal photo opens it).
- `/reading`: no "Log a session" form; Review, highlights (add, Kindle import, edit) and the Session log stay.
- Unchanged: `/check-in` (its own page for the 21:30 card's dated deep links, not in the scroll), the chat write tools and agent cards (other ways to say the same thing, not pages).

A new kind of daily entry goes into a Log section, not onto a domain page. Part ids of the removed forms (`weight-log`, `log-session`, `log-intake`) are kept for the link or the list that replaced them, so saved layouts keep their place. Tests: `frontend/tests/supplements-page.test.cjs`, `frontend/tests/delivery-leftovers.test.cjs` (read-only lists), `frontend/tests/checkin.test.cjs`.
