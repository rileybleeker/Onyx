## Procurement (2026-11-13)

`/procurement` gives **read-only advice, never buys**: no route, function or button places an order, opens a cart, checks out, pays or cancels (ordering / PunchOut was cancelled 2026-11-11; `executionReadiness()` always fails closed; do not revive `feat/procurement-punchout`). Everything is rule-based code: **no model or chat tool reads finance data**. Details: `docs/procurement-offline.md`.

- **Detected subscriptions + renewal alerts.** `lib/procurement-recurring-server.ts` (`server-only`, service role, explicit columns) reads 400 days of `pds.finance_transactions` per request; `lib/procurement-recurring.ts` (pure) groups counted USD purchases by merchant and accepts a cadence when the median gap fits a band (weekly 6-8 d, monthly 26-35, quarterly 84-98, annual 350-380), 70% of gaps are in it and the last three amounts agree within 25%. Each subscription gets an opaque `id` (hash of the normalized merchant), an estimated next charge date, the **method** (calendar = same day of the month, or a fixed interval, whichever fits the last four gaps better; median of the last three charges carried forward) and a **confidence** (high = 3+ gaps, none off by more than 2 days; medium = none off by more than 4; else low). Same-amount charges on no schedule are listed as irregular with **no date**. `lib/procurement-renewals.ts` decides what alerts: active subscriptions with high / medium confidence only. The page (`app/procurement/DetectedSubscriptions.tsx`) shows "Renewing soon" (14 days) and the method + confidence per row; the page stays `force-dynamic` + `noindex`, never ISR.
- **Today "Renewals" panel** renders only when a renewal is due within 3 days: `GET /api/procurement/renewals/summary` (owner session, `no-store`, GET only, nothing written; merchant + "renews tomorrow", **no amounts**; 500 on a failed ledger read, `{status:"empty"}` for an empty ledger). A failed check keeps the last answer and never shows an all-clear.
- **Waste flags** (`lib/procurement-flags.ts` pure + `lib/procurement-flags-server.ts` `server-only`), on ACTIVE subscriptions, each with its reason: **price went up** (last charge at least 5% and $0.50 above the usual amount of the up-to-three charges before it; one step up of at most double keeps the subscription detected), **possible duplicate** (two active subscriptions in one category of `SERVICE_CATEGORIES`, a short hand-edited list: add a service by adding a line), **no recent use on your iPhone** (only when the subscription is matched to an app / site in `pds.phone_usage_items` and the last 30 days total under 10 minutes with at least 20 days of Screen Time data; otherwise the row says "iPhone use: unknown (why)" and nothing is flagged; a failed or cut-off Screen Time read is unknown, never zero). Dismiss / Restore goes through `PATCH /api/procurement/flags` (`{sub, flag, dismissed}`; owner session, same-origin JSON, 256 bytes) into `pds.procurement_flag_dismissals` (`sql/procurement_flag_dismissals.sql`; service-role only; two hashes and a boolean). Flag keys carry the new amount / the other subscriptions, so a later increase or a new duplicate comes back.
- **Purchase suggestions** (`lib/procurement-reorder.ts` pure + `lib/procurement-reorder-server.ts` `server-only`; card `app/procurement/ReorderSuggestions.tsx`): from `pds.online_orders` item names (shipping emails + the Amazon order list, last 540 days; restaurant delivery is NOT read), an item ordered on 3+ separate occasions (orders within 3 days = one occasion) gets its median reorder interval and "likely due" (90% of the interval since the last order) / "due soon" (70%) / "not yet", with the reason; canceled orders never count, items ordered twice or not for 3+ intervals are only counted. The card has **no link, button or form** (a test fails if one is added): never add a cart or store link.
- **Renewal card in `/agents` (Riley's decision 2026-11-13: merchant name yes, amount never).** `POST /api/procurement/renewals/claim` is the **only** procurement path in `isCiRoute()`, exact match; the sms-reminders Worker polls it hourly (quiet 23:30-07:00) and posts "<merchant> renews in N days" (thread `renewals`, card type `renewal`, one "Open procurement" link, no buttons). `lib/procurement-renewal-claim.ts` claims active subscriptions renewing within 3 days (high / medium confidence, same rule as Today) with an atomic `INSERT ... ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING RETURNING` into `pds.procurement_renewal_prompt` (`sql/procurement_renewal_prompts.sql`; service-role only; key = subscription hash + date of last charge = one billing cycle), after everything that can fail. **The response carries only `id`, `merchant`, `renewal_date`, `days`** (`CLAIM_FIELDS`); never add an amount, account, category or transaction id: `tests/procurement-renewal-claim.test.cjs` and `scripts/test-procurement-store.mjs` fail if a field or a second CI path is added. Heartbeat `procurement_renewals|prompt` (code-only error text, never a merchant) and `/status` card **Renewal Reminders** (>3 h degraded, >9 h or failed red). Every other procurement route and every finance route stays session-only.
- Hand-typed tracker (`pds.procurement_subscriptions`, service-role only, `/api/procurement/subscriptions`), `.ics` reminder file and in-memory order planner are unchanged.
- Tests (synthetic data only): `frontend/tests/procurement-recurring.test.cjs`, `frontend/tests/procurement-renewals.test.cjs`, `frontend/tests/procurement-flags.test.cjs`, `frontend/tests/procurement-reorder.test.cjs`, `frontend/tests/procurement-renewal-claim.test.cjs`, `frontend/scripts/test-procurement*.mjs`, `tests/today-regression.cjs`.
