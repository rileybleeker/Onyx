# EXP-900: A/A check, both arms the same

**Why.** Before the first real trial (EXP-001, phone off in the last hour before bed) runs for
months, this four-week run puts the whole pipeline through one complete trial with nothing to
find: the seed and registration, the daily arm on Today, the check-in question, the daily
progress report and its Notion page, the final analysis, the seed reveal and the Notion report.
Both arms say "usual day and evening", so Riley does nothing different on any day.

**What a pass looks like.** The final report (pds.experiment_reports and the "Report (auto)"
section of the EXP-900 page in Onyx Experiments) leads with "A/A check PASSED" when all three hold:

1. the registered analysis finds no difference between the identical arms (randomization
   p >= 0.05). By chance alone about 1 run in 20 fails this item even when everything works;
   that is the test's 5% false-positive rate, so one failure means "look at the pipeline", not
   "the pipeline is broken";
2. every one of the 28 days was revealed on Today (open Today once a day);
3. 90% or more of the days have a check-in answer: 26 or more of the 28.

The decision line will usually read "Inconclusive": four weeks are far too short to rule an
8 ms effect in or out. That is expected and is not what the check is about.

**What Riley does each day.** Open Today (the card shows Arm A or Arm B, same instruction) and
answer "Followed today's arm?" in the check-in. Wear the WHOOP every night.

**Afterwards.** One trial can be active at a time, so EXP-001 is registered only once this
check is complete.
