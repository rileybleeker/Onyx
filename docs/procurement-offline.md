# Procurement: read-only advice

Route: `/procurement`. Read-only advice from Riley's own data plus a hand-typed tracker and an order planner. It is not a purchasing integration: nothing here buys, pays, cancels, opens a cart or checks out (ordering / PunchOut was cancelled on 2026-11-11 and stays cancelled).

## Detected subscriptions and renewal alerts (2026-11-13)
- **Source.** `lib/procurement-recurring-server.ts` (`server-only`, service role, explicit column list) reads the last 400 days of `pds.finance_transactions` on every page load; `lib/procurement-recurring.ts` (pure) finds recurring charges by fixed rules. No model reads finance data. The page is `force-dynamic` and `noindex`; nothing is cached.
- **Cadence.** Charges are grouped by merchant (store numbers stripped); the median gap must fall in a band (weekly 6-8 days, monthly 26-35, quarterly 84-98, annual 350-380), 70% of gaps must be in it, and the last three amounts must be within 25% of each other. Refunds, ignored rows, transfers and non-USD rows never count.
- **Next charge date.** Two models are tried on the last four gaps and the better fit is used: *calendar* (same day of the month, 1 / 3 / 12 months on) or *interval* (a fixed number of days, e.g. every 28 days). The date is the median of the last three charges carried forward, so one charge that posted late over a weekend does not move it; billing on the 29th-31st is anchored to the latest day of the month seen.
- **Confidence** says how well that model reproduced the recent charges: *high* = at least 3 gaps checked, none off by more than 2 days; *medium* = none off by more than 4 days (an annual charge seen twice can be medium at best); *low* = anything else. The page prints the method and the confidence under every subscription.
- **Irregular repeats get no date.** A merchant charged at least 4 times for the same amount (80% of charges within 5%) at uneven intervals is listed under "repeating charges without a regular schedule" with no next date.
- **On the page.** "Renewing soon" lists every active subscription expected in the next 14 days (and one expected up to 7 days ago that has not posted yet); each row says "renews in N days". A low-confidence date is marked as rough.
- **On Today.** A small "Renewals" panel renders only when a high- or medium-confidence renewal is due within 3 days. It reads `GET /api/procurement/renewals/summary` (owner session, `no-store`, GET only, nothing written or marked): merchant and "renews tomorrow", no amounts. A failed check keeps the last answer; an empty ledger hides the panel.
- **Push card in /agents.** A few days before a renewal the sms-reminders Worker posts "<merchant> renews in N days" in the Agents chat. See "Renewal card in /agents" below.

## Waste flags (2026-11-13)
Rule-based flags on every ACTIVE detected subscription, each shown with its reason under the subscription it belongs to (`lib/procurement-flags.ts`, pure; `lib/procurement-flags-server.ts`, `server-only`). No model is involved.
- **Price went up.** The last charge is above the usual amount of the (up to three) charges before it by at least 5% **and** at least $0.50. The reason prints both amounts and the percentage. A jump of more than 25% no longer drops the subscription from detection: one step up of at most double is still the same subscription, and the new amount is what it costs now.
- **Possible duplicate.** Two or more active subscriptions fall in the same service category. The category list is `SERVICE_CATEGORIES` in `lib/procurement-flags.ts`: a short, explicit list of well-known services (video, music, AI assistants, cloud storage, news, meditation, language learning, delivery memberships). To add a service, add one line (`name`, `match` words, optional `apps` bundle-id prefixes and `sites`). A merchant that matches nothing gets no duplicate flag.
- **No recent use on your iPhone**, only where data backs it. The subscription is matched to Screen Time (`pds.phone_usage_segments` / `pds.phone_usage_items`, service-role, server-side): by the service's `apps` / `sites`, or, for a merchant outside the list, by a distinctive word of its name that is a part of a bundle id or domain present in the data. It is flagged when the time over the last 30 days is under 10 minutes and Screen Time has data on at least 20 of those days. The reason says how much time, in which app, and that use on a computer or TV is not visible to Onyx. Everything else reads **"iPhone use: unknown (why)"** and raises no flag: no Screen Time data, thin coverage, a service that is not used through an app (storage), a name too generic to match, or no matching app. A cut-off or failed Screen Time read is unknown, never zero.
- **Dismissing.** Each flag has a Dismiss button; dismissed flags move to a "dismissed flags" list with Restore. `PATCH /api/procurement/flags` (`{sub, flag, dismissed}`; owner session, same-origin JSON, 256-byte cap, not in the CI carve-out) writes one row of `pds.procurement_flag_dismissals` (`sql/procurement_flag_dismissals.sql`, applied 2026-11-13; service-role only; two opaque hashes and a boolean, no merchant or amount). A dismissal sticks across visits. The key of a price flag includes the new amount and the key of a duplicate flag includes the other subscriptions, so a later increase or a new duplicate shows again. If the table cannot be read every flag is shown and the buttons are hidden.

## Purchase suggestions (2026-11-13)
"Usually reordered every ~N days, last ordered D, likely due", with the reason, for things bought repeatedly (`lib/procurement-reorder.ts`, pure; `lib/procurement-reorder-server.ts`, `server-only`; card `app/procurement/ReorderSuggestions.tsx`).
- **Source.** `pds.online_orders` (the order history behind `/orders`: shipping emails and the personal Amazon order list), last 540 days, item names + store + dates only (that table has no prices). Restaurant delivery is out of scope and its tables are not read. No model is involved.
- **Rules.** The same item name (case, spacing and punctuation ignored) on at least **3 separate occasions**; orders within 3 days of each other are one occasion (split shipment, or one order seen twice). The interval is the **median gap**; under 7 days is not a reorder pattern. **Likely due** from 90% of the interval since the last order, **due soon** from 70%, otherwise "not yet" (folded away). Uneven gaps (spread over half the interval) are called a rough figure. Canceled orders never count. An item not ordered for more than 3 intervals is treated as stopped and only counted, as are items ordered exactly twice.
- **Advice only.** The card has no link, button or form (a test enforces it): nothing leads to a store, a cart or a checkout.
- **Limits.** Order emails list at most five items per order, so an item can be missing from a large order; two listings of one product with different names are two items.
- States: suggestions / history read but no repeats / no orders with item names / order history not set up / failed query are five different messages.

## Renewal card in /agents (2026-11-13)
Riley's decision of 2026-11-13: raise a card in the Agents chat before a renewal, **with the merchant name and no amount** ("X renews in 2 days"). Amounts on Today stay off.
- **Route.** `POST /api/procurement/renewals/claim` (`{now?}`), called by the sms-reminders Worker once an hour (quiet 23:30-07:00) with the `x-onyx-ci-token` header. It is the **one** procurement path in `isCiRoute()`, matched exactly; every other procurement route and everything else over the finance ledger stays session-only. `scripts/test-procurement-store.mjs` allows exactly this path and fails on a second one or a prefix.
- **What is claimed.** The same rule as the Today panel: an active subscription whose next charge is estimated within 3 days with high or medium confidence. At most 10 per call.
- **What leaves Onyx.** Per renewal: `id` (an opaque hash of the billing cycle), `merchant` (display name, one line, 80 characters), `renewal_date`, `days`. Nothing else: no amount, account, category, transaction id or charge date. `tests/procurement-renewal-claim.test.cjs` fails if the response carries any other field or an amount value.
- **Prompted once.** `pds.procurement_renewal_prompt` (`sql/procurement_renewal_prompts.sql`, applied 2026-11-13; service-role only; subscription hash, date of the last charge, the estimate; no merchant, no amount), primary key (subscription, last charge) = one billing cycle. The claim is `INSERT ... ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING RETURNING`, so two polls at once, or an estimate that moves by a day, cannot prompt a cycle twice; the next cycle starts when the next charge posts. The ledger read, the lookup of cycles already prompted and the card text all happen before that insert.
- **Heartbeat.** `procurement_renewals|prompt` in `pds.sync_log` on every call that claims something and otherwise at most every 50 minutes; a failed call writes a failed row whose text is a step and a database error code. `/status` card **Renewal Reminders** (badge AGENTS): more than 3 h without a poll degraded, more than 9 h or a failed heartbeat red. Neither the heartbeat nor any log line carries a merchant.
- **The card** (Worker repo, `src/lib/renewals.ts`): thread `renewals`, type `renewal`, name "<merchant> renews in N days", link `/procurement`; push title "Renewal", body the same sentence. Informational: `/agents` shows one "Open procurement" link and no reply button (`cardActions` in `app/agents/agent-ui.tsx`). It expires the day after the estimated date or is cleared from Unread. The Worker reads only the four fields above and keeps renewal cards out of what its assistant model sees.
- **Known limit.** A claim is consumed when Onyx answers. If the Worker then fails to post the card it is lost for that billing cycle; the Today panel still shows the renewal.

## What else works
- Navigation through the shared desktop/mobile/palette catalog (key hint `O`).
- "What this page reads" says where the numbers come from and that no merchant is connected; opt-in synthetic examples, generic buying checklist.
- **Subscription tracker (typed by hand).** Name, USD price, monthly/quarterly/annual billing, next renewal date. Shows approximate monthly and yearly cost, renewals sorted by date (a past date is rolled forward by whole billing cycles, month-end safe, leap-day safe), and plain-words "worth a look" prompts: renewing within 14 days, the same name tracked twice, one item over half the spend. "Load synthetic demo subscriptions" fills it with four obviously fake rows.
- **Renewal reminders.** "Download renewal reminders (.ics)" builds an all-day calendar file (reminder 1, 3, 7 or 14 days before each renewal, never in the past) in the browser. Riley imports it into his own calendar; Onyx sends nothing and calls no calendar API. The file is deterministic, escapes names and folds long lines per RFC 5545.
- **Order preparation.** In-memory USD order drafts with exact-cent validation, one-time/monthly commitment, removal and clear. Totals (one-time and recurring), a warning when a recurring draft matches a tracked subscription, and a "Prepared list" grouped by merchant that Riley can copy. The list says in its first lines that nothing was ordered and that he buys it himself.
- **Saved tracker (2026-11-11).** The subscription tracker persists to `pds.procurement_subscriptions` (name, vendor, amount in cents, currency, cadence, next renewal, notes, `is_active` soft delete, timestamps) through owner-session API routes `/api/procurement/subscriptions` (GET list, POST create, DELETE archive all) and `/api/procurement/subscriptions/[id]` (PATCH update, DELETE archive one). The table is service-role only (`sql/procurement_schema.sql`, mirrors `phone_usage_schema.sql`); the browser never reads it directly and the routes are not on any middleware carve-out. The form has no vendor/notes inputs yet; the API accepts them. Synthetic demo rows and order drafts are never saved.
- **Graceful fallback.** If the table is missing (SQL not applied yet) or the API is unreachable, the page keeps working in memory and shows a visible note ("Not saved yet - setup pending ..." or "Saved subscriptions could not be loaded right now ..."). Reads degrade to an empty list with `persisted:false`; writes answer 503 `setupPending:true` and the page keeps the row in memory. A real database error is never reported as "setup pending".
- No localStorage, merchant calls, account discovery, credential entry, or external product recommendations. Order drafts still disappear on reload. Provider management and ordering remain disabled. `executionReadiness()` takes no arguments and always fails closed. There is no purchase, checkout, order-submission or cancel route or function; "archive" only hides a row in Onyx.
- `/status` has a small manual "Procurement" card: grey (unknown) with "saving not set up" until the SQL is applied, green once the table answers, red only on a real database error. Data age is not a signal for a hand-typed list.

## One-time setup (Riley)
Open the Supabase SQL editor for the Onyx project and run `sql/procurement_schema.sql` (idempotent, no DROP). Reload `/procurement`: the note under "Track subscriptions" changes to "Saved to your Onyx account".

## Future execution boundary (not implemented)
An authenticated server must own every quote and authorization, never trust a client approval boolean. The immutable quote needs merchant identity, item/quantity, delivered total including all fees, currency, recurrence/renewal price, fulfillment destination/method, cancellation/refund restrictions, and opaque payment-method reference. Never store card numbers or secrets here.

Authorization must bind the owner, quote revision and exact terms, have a short expiry, and be revoked on any material change. Before execution, revalidate current quote, limits and provider connection. Enforce a server-side idempotency key and atomic execution lock. Record pending, confirmed, failed and unknown outcomes separately. After a timeout, reconcile the provider transaction before any retry. Maintain an audit trail without secrets. Require provider-specific cancellation confirmation and verify the effective date rather than presenting a local state change as cancellation. A draft is not authorization.

No live adapter or authorization endpoint exists in this patch; a UI flag cannot enable spending. Recommendation adapters must return evidence, price timestamps and availability, and distinguish stale, unavailable and empty data. Keep recommendations independent of checkout and label sponsored content if introduced.

The finance ledger is read only by the server loader named above, never by the browser, a chat tool or a model.
